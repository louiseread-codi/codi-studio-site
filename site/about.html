About Louise

Louise Read is the founder of Codi Studio, an AI consultancy for established businesses.

She works with CEOs and leadership teams to find where AI will have a commercial impact, then implements it across the business.

She has been building systems for businesses for more than a decade, starting with marketing automations for a FTSE 100 company, long before anyone was talking about AI.

For the past five years she has worked in AI, helping leading AI companies go to market and taking some of the first AI agents to market in 2024. After years helping people adopt AI, she sees the real value in redesigning entire businesses around it.

Louise is a regular speaker and panellist on AI, and has been featured as a voice on AI and technology by the Daily Mail, Computer Weekly, Business Leader, Startups Magazine and TechRound.

Why Louise set up Codi Studio

Only 37% of businesses report any profit impact from AI (McKinsey, The State of AI 2026). Louise saw why, business after business. They had given everyone licences, some had paid for training, and they told her they were using AI everywhere. But nothing had been automated in a way that showed up on the P&L, and different teams were building the same things, so the business was paying for them twice.

Codi Studio exists to change that. We redesign businesses for the AI era, with an AI workforce of agents and automations working alongside the team. The result is a business that does more and costs less to run, with AI showing up where it matters: lower costs, higher margins and more profit.

Our vision

Every business run by people and AI together, and more profitable for it.

Our mission

To help established businesses find where AI will have a commercial impact and implement it, while keeping what makes them unique.

What we at Codi Studio believe

Most businesses have bought AI licences and seen the cost go up, with nothing to show on the P&L. AI has become a cost centre.

AI is commoditising intelligence, and the businesses that win this era will be the ones that redesign how they work around it, led from the top. We call that the orchestrated business: human-led and AI-operated, with governance built in from the start, and what makes the business unique protected.

What makes Codi Studio different

Knowing the business first

Designed around people

AI should fit how each team works. We protect what clients would never hand over: their voice, their key relationships and the parts of the job they love.

Safe from the start

Good AI habits and an AI use policy from day one.

Leaving teams confident

We explain as we go and share a clear plan, so everyone knows how AI is used and why, and can run what we build.
