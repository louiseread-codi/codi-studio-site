Choose a Partnership package that fits you

Whole Business Partnership

For leadership teams that know they need AI and want it implemented centrally, across the whole business.

From £7,500 a monthfor 12 months
How it is delivered:
  • ✓AI Foundations. A two-hour AI Foundations session for your leadership team, so they are using AI safely and confidently from day one, with our AI use policy template tailored to your business.
  • ✓Evaluate. One-hour interviews with up to eight department leads, and entire workflows and processes mapped for up to six departments, so we can see which are ready for automation.
  • ✓Roadmap. Every opportunity scored and prioritised by commercial impact. An executive roadmap report, and a session with your leadership team to agree the priority builds and the delivery timeline.
  • ✓Build. 90-minute AI build sessions every two weeks with your department champions, building the automations and agents live together, with support for them in between.
  • ✓Roll out. Quarterly training sessions to upskill teams on the builds.
  • ✓Measure. A quarterly AI impact assessment, and a year-end review.

Department Partnership

For the business that wants one department running on AI first, done properly.

From £5,400 a monthfor 6 months
How it is delivered:
  • ✓AI Foundations. A two-hour AI Foundations session for the department, so they are using AI safely and confidently from day one, with our AI use policy template tailored to the department.
  • ✓Evaluate. One-hour interviews with the department lead and up to four team leads, and the department's workflows and processes mapped in full.
  • ✓Roadmap. Every opportunity scored and prioritised by commercial impact. A roadmap report, and a session to agree the priority builds and the delivery timeline.
  • ✓Build. 90-minute AI build sessions every two weeks with your department champion, building the automations and agents live together, with support for them in between.
  • ✓Roll out. Quarterly training sessions to upskill the department on the builds.
  • ✓Measure. A quarterly AI impact assessment, and a final report at six months.

Executive Partnership

For the individual who wants to get ahead before involving the whole team.

From £2,400 a monthfor 6 months
Delivered as 90-minute 1:1 sessions, with support in between.
  • ✓AI Foundations. Your first session gets you using AI safely and confidently.
  • ✓Evaluate. Your own workflows and processes mapped in full, so we can see which are ready for automation.
  • ✓Roadmap. Every opportunity scored and prioritised by commercial impact, and an executive roadmap agreed with you, with the builds in order.
  • ✓Build. Building alongside you from the first session, working down your roadmap.
  • ✓Measure. A final AI impact assessment at six months.
