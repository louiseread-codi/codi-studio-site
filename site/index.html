Technology isn't the problem

Businesses have bought AI licences and upskilled their people, yet they're not seeing anything change on the P&L, while the licence bill continues to grow. The leadership team knows AI matters, but doesn't know how to implement it strategically.

Getting a return on AI starts with knowing where it will drive commercial value, what that value is worth, and how you'll measure it against the P&L. That's what we help you find.

Data protection, built in

Worried about data protection? Many leadership teams tell us they can't use AI in their business because of it. We work with you to set AI up the right way, with the right guardrails in place, including audit processes, so your business can use it safely.

Business first, AI second

Being an AI expert isn't enough. You need a partner who truly understands business.

codi studio brings commercial judgement to AI. We get to know your business first and agree with you where AI will drive commercial value, and where it won't, before anything gets built.

Protect what makes you different

In five years' time, every business will run on AI and agents. Alongside that, sometimes the most valuable thing you can do is protect what makes you different. In the AI era, your uniqueness becomes your competitive advantage.

We design AI around the people who use it and the customers who feel it. Some things should never be automated, and we help you decide what should and shouldn't be.

Built with your people, owned in-house

Every automation and agent is built in live sessions with a champion from your team, so your people stay in control and the knowledge stays in the business. Then we train everyone who needs it, so what gets built gets used.
